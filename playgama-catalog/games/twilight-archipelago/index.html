About Twilight Archipelago

A voxel pixel-art adventure across nine islands at dusk: a red canyon, a blast crater, a snowy mountain, a volcano, sea stacks, an atoll, a jungle ziggurat and a crystal rift. Swim between them, collect coins and light the lighthouse on every island while a giant whale hunts you from the deep, breaching in slow motion and crashing down where you swim.

How to play Twilight Archipelago

Light the lighthouses on all eight islands to bring the dawn. Move: WASD or arrows (left finger on phones), camera: drag the mouse (right finger). Space or the yellow button jumps, J / click / orange button strikes; in water it is a dash stroke that costs stamina, which recovers only on land. When a dark shadow appears on the water, the whale is coming; when its rim glows red, swim out of it fast. P opens the menu.
