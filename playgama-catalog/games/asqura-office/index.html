About ASQURA Office

A pixel-art office manager game with a time-travelling lift. Keep your team working: hand out tasks, wake up sleepyheads and pour coffee before the day timer runs out and the office mood hits zero. Earn salary, buy upgrades, ride the lift to a royal court in 1750 or a monster lab in 2099, each with its own crisis, and survive SWAT raids. Can ASQURA last 30 days?

How to play ASQURA Office

Tap a worker with a bubble over their head: «!» gives them a task, a cup sends them for coffee, «Z» wakes them up, a wrench sends them to fix the printer. Finish the day's tasks before the timer ends and don't let the mood bar drop to zero. Spend salary on upgrades between days. Tap the lift to travel to another era. During a raid, tap a worker and pick run, hide or attack, or call the lift. Drag to look around. Survive 30 days to win.
