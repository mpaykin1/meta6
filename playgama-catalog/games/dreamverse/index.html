About Dreamverse (all games in one)

A regular ASQURA office hides unusual secrets. Employees have strange dreams, and the office elevator moves them through time: 1750, 2026, 2099. In every era someone attacks the office, and after travelling everyone forgets what happened. Manage your team, tap sleeping workers to see their dreams, collect clues and uncover who is behind the attacks and who is actually sleeping.

How to play Dreamverse (all games in one)

Tap workers to give them tasks, coffee or wake them up; drag to move the camera. Tap a sleeping worker (Z) to watch their dream and get a clue. Use the time elevator to travel between 1750, 2026 and 2099. During an attack, tap a worker and choose run, hide or throw an object, or call the elevator to escape. Collect all 8 clues to solve the mystery.
