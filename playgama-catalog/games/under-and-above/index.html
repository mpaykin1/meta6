About Under and Above

Explore voxel islands above the sea and a vast sunken city below it. Collect coins and pearls, discover weapons and relics, and fight the guardians to relight the lighthouse. Choose a Wanderer or a lantern-carrying Keeper, brave a frozen fjord and changing weather, and dive beneath the waves to find cathedral ruins, sea creatures and a bathyscaphe. Swim back to the surface with your equipment. Progress and personal records are saved.

How to play Under and Above

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Press Space to jump; hold it underwater to swim upward. Attack with the left mouse button or J. Switch weapons with Q/E or number keys and reload with R. Drag with the right mouse button to move the camera, or use Z/C to rotate it. Escape opens the pause menu and arsenal. On mobile, move with the left thumb, turn the camera with the right thumb, and use the attack, jump and weapon buttons. Collect every coin, defeat the guardian and approach the lighthouse. Enter deep water to explore the sunken city.
