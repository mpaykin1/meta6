About Foggy Awakening

A dark third-person horror action game in a foggy voxel world. You wake up in the mist and walk toward a glowing tree while silhouettes, crawlers, bone warriors and deep-sea creatures hunt you through the fog. Collect light shards, find 28 weapons from swords and scythes to a tesla staff and a prism beam, master triple jumps, rolls and perfect dodges, and face the Tree Guardian dragon.

How to play Foggy Awakening

Reach the glowing tree and collect the light shards on the way. Desktop: WASD or arrows to move, Shift to run, mouse to look, hold F or the left mouse button to attack, Space to jump (press again in the air for up to 3 jumps), Q to roll, 1-7, mouse wheel or Tab to switch weapons, R to reload, H to heal, E to enter cabins. Mobile: left joystick to move, drag on the right to look, big button to attack, arrow to jump, circle to roll. Roll right before an enemy hits for a perfect dodge and double damage, attack in the air to slam the ground.
