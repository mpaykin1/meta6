About Endless Valleys

A 3D action adventure in a grey voxel world that has lost its colour. You play a knight in flaking gold-leaf armour. Fight unique gilded creatures in the fog: every one is generated differently and none ever repeats. They turn to colour as they die and leave a memory behind. Carry the memories to seven altars, defeat their guardians and give the valleys back their colours one by one, while your own gold slowly falls away.

How to play Endless Valleys

Goal: follow the pillar of light to each altar, defeat its guardian and offer the colour memories you collect from fallen creatures. Your gold leaf is your health. Desktop: WASD to walk, Shift to run, Space to jump, left click or J to strike, hold K or the left mouse button to block (block on impact to parry), L to roll, U I O Y T for flips and spins, right mouse drag or Q/E to turn the camera, P to pause. Mobile: left half of the screen to walk, right half: tap to strike, swipe for acrobatic moves, hold to block, two fingers to turn the camera.
