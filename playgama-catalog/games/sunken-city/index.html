About Sunken City

Pilot a brass bathyscaphe over an endless voxel seabed of drowned gothic towns, ruins and shipwreck fields. Ping the sonar to reveal hidden relics as columns of light, carry them back to the old bell tower in the city and upgrade your sub with every delivery. Anglers hunt you harder the farther you roam, and your hull can give way.

How to play Sunken City

Goal: find relics with the sonar and deliver them to the bell tower in the city centre; 30 relics wake the tower bell. PC: WASD to swim, Space or R to rise, Shift or F to dive, arrow keys or mouse drag to look around, mouse wheel to zoom, E for sonar, M for the menu. Phone: left thumb swims, right thumb looks around, up and down buttons change depth, the round button fires the sonar. The sonar also stuns anglers; your hull is repaired at the tower.
