About Broken City

Start small in a city that bends up over your head. Buildings collapse into improvised bridges, giant toys spill from the ruins, and shadows creep closer whenever you look away. Each stage introduces new movement and survival mechanics as the city's mystery unfolds.

How to play Broken City

Reach the light at the end of each street. On phones, use the left-side joystick to move, swipe on the right to look, and tap the action buttons. On desktop, use WASD or arrow keys to move, Shift to run, Space to jump, E to stomp, Q to lower bridges or topple houses, P to pause, and the mouse to look. Stand near glowing TVs to recharge your lamp.
