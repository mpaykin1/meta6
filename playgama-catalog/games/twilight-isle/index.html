About Twilight Isle

A voxel island action game at dusk. Wash ashore empty-handed, collect every coin, arm yourself with weapons scattered around the island and defeat the Lighthouse Warden before the shadows take over. Three islands to choose from: warm Twilight Island, the snowy Frozen Fjord and the grey wasteland of Ash Road, each with its own boss, weather and enemies. Over 20 weapons from swords and bows to plasma guns and boomerangs, plus relics with passive powers.

How to play Twilight Isle

Collect all coins on the island, find weapons, survive the night and defeat the boss to light the lighthouse. Desktop: WASD to move, Space to jump, left mouse or J to attack, Q/E or 1-0 to switch weapons, R to reload, right mouse to rotate the camera, Esc for the arsenal. Mobile: left thumb moves, right thumb turns the camera, on-screen buttons attack, jump and switch weapons.
