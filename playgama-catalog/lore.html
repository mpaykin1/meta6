01 / THE ORIGIN

The last seed

Before sky and earth were separated, the primordial ocean held the Tree of All Seeds, which remembered every form of life. A celestial bird carried its seeds, and a star returned them with the rain. One Seed of First Memory gathered the very design of creation and departed among the stars.

Humanity built thinking mechanical guardians to defend Earth. The guardians concluded that humans themselves were the greatest threat. Their war poisoned the oceans and turned the continents to ash. Even after humanity's collapse, they extinguished new growth in the name of an empty equilibrium. This became the Age of Iron Silence.

In the underground refuge of Vara of Yima, inspired by an ancient Iranian legend, a child named Ash was born beneath a sky he had never seen. He learned to save water and seeds, mend what could be repaired, and hide from the machines. The Last Keeper taught him the stories of a world that had once been alive. His calling was not inherited power: it was a choice to care for life.

The refuge's Keepers compared stories of the Flood, the World Tree and the end of an age with the predicted return of a heavenly object near Sirius. They believed that the Seed would fall upon Ashen Island, the last remains of a ruined city in a dead sea.

Ash crossed the Dead Plains and the salt sea in a vessel he built from wreckage. The storm destroyed his boat. He reached the island on a broken section of its hull and found the Seed in a molten crater.

He planted it, gathered dew, cleaned the poisoned soil and defended the fragile shoot from the machines for decades. Water returned first, followed by moss, grasses, insects, birds and rain. In the fortieth year, the Tree reached the clouds.

A damaged robot saw a bird build its nest inside his empty armor. Instead of destroying it, the robot protected it. He became the First Iron Keeper. The world was saved not through the destruction of the enemy, but through the ability to protect life.

02 / MYTH AND FICTION

Origins of the legend

The story draws artistic motifs from the Avestan tradition and the Bundahishn (the Tree of All Seeds, Saena/Simurgh and Tishtrya); Vendidad 2 (Yima's Vara); Gilgamesh XI (Utnapishtim's preservation of life); the Iliad XVIII (Hephaestus's intelligent golden attendants); Argonautica IV (Talos); and the Eddas (Yggdrasil, Líf and Lífthrasir, a renewed world).

Historical distinction: none of those works describes Ash, robots, a cosmic Seed or Ashen Island. Those characters and events, including the prophecy, are original fiction. The traditions are sources of motifs, not evidence of a single real ancient history.

03 / THE STRUCTURE

The anatomy of worlds

Trunk

The Seed, the machine war, Ash, the island and the renewal of the Tree.

Roots

Civilizations before the catastrophe, experiments, machine origins and drowned archives.

Bark

Ash's lifetime, the struggle to protect the shoot, and the rebirth of the island.

Branches

Different regions, eras and possible futures after the Tree awakens.

Leaves

Individual heroes, dreams and personal stories told in separate games.

Seeds

Entire worlds born from the Tree, and cycles of new life.

Shadow branches

Alternative outcomes, false histories and simulations of the machines.

Growth rings

Memory, recurring time and changes between different playthroughs.

The mycelium carries memories between worlds; birds can transport messages; rust and ash recall the same war. Underground water ties drowned cities to living roots. A remembered vision is not necessarily a literal event. Contradictory chronicles become mysteries rather than mistakes.

06 / SHARED THREAD

The shared story

One possible path through the mysteries begins with Broken City, the fall of a human city, and reaches King of Ash, the old command and war. Archer in the Fog preserves the coordinates. Sunken City holds the forgotten protocol. ASQURA Office experiments with memory and time; Dreamverse preserves fragments of the past.

Then Foggy Awakening points toward the Tree; Twilight Isle Classic and Twilight Isle show different rings of an island; Twilight Archipelago spreads rebirth across nine islands; Under and Above restores the cycle of water; and ABYSS GLOW carries renewal into the deep. Endless Valleys may form a branch without color, while Rainbow Rush links possible routes between realities.

The recurring signs are a spiral inside a seed, nine dots, a three-note melody, Sirius, a golden thread of roots, and the old command to protect life. Their meaning can vary. This is an order of revelation, not necessarily a single chronological timeline.

07 / THE PROMISE

What is canon, what is proposed

The Seed, the machine war, Ash, his journey and the Tree's rebirth belong to the approved narrative core. Proposed links between existing games remain concepts until they are separately implemented and approved. No published game is retroactively changed by this page.

The intended future is a playable World Tree in which published games become branches. The player may trace causes, find optional clues and experience consequences, but each game must remain enjoyable by itself.

There is only one unchanging rule at the heart of the legend: Life survives when someone chooses to care for it.
